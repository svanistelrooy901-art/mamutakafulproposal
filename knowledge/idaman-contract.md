# A-Life Idaman + riders: contract facts (verified)

Source: contract clause pages supplied by Mamu, 7 Oct 2026 (no client data).
Form refs: S022615B09 (base, 04/25–06/26 pages) · A-Plus Health360-i T1088T (06/26) · A-Plus DisabilityCare-i T1058T (08/21).

## Base plan: A-Life Idaman
- Benefits in the base plan: **Death, Accidental Death, Maturity**. TPD is NOT in the base plan.
- Death (after 30 days from birth): sum covered (from PRF) + PAF account value + PIF account value + RM5,000.
  - The RM5,000 is for Badal Hajj if Muslim and aged 16+; otherwise it is paid as funeral expenses.
- Death before birth / within 30 days of birth: the higher of total contributions paid or PAF value, plus PIF.
- Accidental death (before age 70): death benefit + extra 100% of sum covered.
  - Exclusions: self-harm, war or riot, unlawful acts, drugs or alcohol, non-commercial aviation.
- Maturity: PAF + PIF account values.
- Auto-extension at maturity: continues to the certificate anniversary at age 100 if account value covers Tabarru' + monthly service charge.
  - Riders also continue to their max age, except Waiver-i and ParentWaiver-i.
  - The client can opt out in writing.
- 5-year no-lapse ("Perlindungan Berterusan Lima Tahun"): stays in force during the first 5 certificate years even if account value is short, provided:
  - contributions are paid on time (or within the grace or late-payment period),
  - there is no withdrawal or change that increases Tabarru'.
- Free-look: 15 days. Refund = account values + Wakalah fee + Tabarru' + charges.
- Grace period: 31 days, followed by a 14-day late-payment period.
- Suicide within 1 year: account values only.
- Contestability: 2 years.
- Surplus: up to 50% to the operator (Ju'alah). The rest goes to participants if no claim, and can be donated to charity.
- Insurable interest: spouse, child, minor under the participant's care, employee, dependant.

## Rider: A-Plus DisabilityCare-i (T1058T): this is where TPD comes from
- Pays the rider sum covered on Presumed Disability or TPD (Suitable Occupation definition: 6 months continuous, unable to do any suitable work).
- **Advance payment**: the base sum covered reduces by the rider sum after a claim. The rider then ends.
- Ends at the anniversary after age 70.
- Excludes congenital conditions diagnosed before 17, plus the standard disability exclusions (incl. pre-existing).

- **Cost-saving options:**
  - RM500 deductible per year (items 1–8).
  - RM500 deductible with SMART Option: off the SMART journey, add 20% co-takaful, capped at RM20,000/yr.
  - Neither applies to emergency or accident treatment, or to government facilities.
- **Waiting periods:**
  - 30 days for any condition (except covered injury).
  - 120 days for specified illnesses: hypertension, diabetes, cardiovascular, growths/cancer, stones, ENT, hernia/haemorrhoids, reproductive, spine/knee.
  - Pre-existing conditions are excluded.
  - Personal Medical Case Management has a 30-day wait.
- Overseas: not covered if outside Malaysia for more than 90 consecutive days (except Singapore and Brunei). Overseas treatment is limited to the Malaysian-equivalent cost.
- Renewable yearly. Tabarru' is not guaranteed (30 days' notice).
- **Not in the contract:** AIA Vitality rewards (Hospitalisation Care, R&B upgrade, Wallet Booster, Healthy Retirement Bonus). These are programme benefits from the brochure; label them as such.
