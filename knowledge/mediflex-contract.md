# A-Life MediFlex-i: contract facts (verified)

Source: contract clause pages supplied by Mamu, 7 Oct 2026 (no client data). Form ref S018052B03 (benefit pages 12/25, general 03/25).
Not yet in the webapp or the skill; waiting for Mamu's go-ahead and the brochure (entry age, expiry age, contribution rates).

## What it is
- A **standalone basic** regular-contribution Medical & Health Takaful certificate, not a rider. It's a pure medical card: no account value, no investment fund, **no Health Wallet**.
- Renewable yearly. The contribution is not guaranteed (30 days' notice) and applies to all regardless of claims.
- Expiry date and contribution-cease date are shown on the Certificate Information Page (not in these clauses).

## Schedule of benefits

| Item | Plan 180 | Plan 250 | Plan 350 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual limit (items 4–14) | RM180,000 | RM250,000 | RM350,000 |
| Lifetime limit | none | none | none |
| Room & board / day (max 150 days per cert year) | RM180 | RM250 | RM350 |
| Extra R&B while overseas / day (combined with the above; max 150 days) | RM180 | RM250 | RM350 |
| OP illness treatment / yr (bronchitis, dengue, influenza, pneumonia only) | RM3,000 | RM4,000 | RM6,000 |
| Home nursing per confinement (180 days lifetime) | RM3,000 | RM4,000 | RM6,000 |

All three plans have the following, as charged within the annual limit:
- ICU (max 150 days per year; beyond that, paid at the R&B rate).
- Hospital supplies & services: nursing, drugs, x-ray, lab, ECG, physio, traditional alternative treatment, IV, blood, domestic ambulance.
- Surgical, operating theatre and anaesthetist fees.
- Physician visits (2 per day per physician).
- Daily guardian for a person covered under 15 or over 60 (150 days).
- Pre-hospitalisation **60 days**: tests, consultation, medication.
- Post-hospitalisation **150 days**: tests, specialist, medication, physio, chiro, acupuncture.
- OP kidney dialysis: 30-day wait; end-stage kidney failure only.
- OP cancer treatment: 60-day wait; must follow a hospital confinement or surgery.
- Day care procedures & surgery.
- Emergency accident OP within 48 hours, incl. dental, + 30 days follow-up.
- Emergency medical evacuation & repatriation: up to USD1M per event.
- Organ / bone marrow transplant as the recipient: once per lifetime.

## Cost-saving options (chosen at application)
- **Option 1: deductible.**
  - Options 1(a), 1(b), 1(d) are fixed deductibles, either per disability or per certificate year.
  - Options 1(c) and 1(e) are **RM20,000 or RM50,000 deductible per year**. If the client opts in at application, it auto-converts to RM500 per disability at age 60 with no underwriting.
  - Good fit for clients who already have an employer medical card (top-up).
- **Option 2: co-takaful.** % of the bill, capped per disability or per year.
- **Option 3: RM500 deductible with SMART Option, per Any One Disability** (the clause sample uses this).
  - Off the SMART journey: + 20% co-takaful on items 4–9 and 11, capped at RM20,000 per disability.
  - Not applied for emergencies or accidents, post-hosp cancer/dialysis, or government facilities.
  - Note the difference from Health360-i: here it is **per disability**, while Health360-i is per year.

## Waiting periods & key exclusions
- **Waiting periods:**
  - 30 days for any condition (except covered injury).
  - 120 days for specified illnesses: the same list as Health360-i (hypertension, diabetes, cardio, tumours/cysts/polyps, stones, ENT, hernia, haemorrhoids, reproductive, spine/knee).
- Pre-existing illness excluded.
- Congenital conditions diagnosed before 17 excluded.
- **Pregnancy/childbirth excluded.**
- **Mental/nervous disorders excluded.**
- Cosmetic surgery excluded.
- Optical excluded, except monofocal IOL in cataract surgery.
- Hearing aids and prostheses excluded.
- Dental excluded, except for accidents.
- Preventive care and check-ups excluded.
- Self-harm, war, and unlawful acts excluded.
- **Overseas:**
  - Treatment abroad is limited to the Malaysian-equivalent cost.
  - No cover if the person stays abroad more than 90 consecutive days (except Singapore and Brunei).
  - Overseas extra R&B only applies if the person was abroad no more than 1 month before admission.
- **Coordination of benefits:** pays only the balance after any other insurance, employer scheme or government compensation.

## Admin
- Free-look: 15 days. Refund = contributions less medical exam costs. Not available if a claim was made.
- Grace period: 31 days, then a 14-day late-payment window. After that the certificate lapses and has no value.
- Surrender: unutilised Tabarru' + unearned Wakalah are refunded on a sliding scale by payment mode, if there was no claim that year. Monthly mode gets no refund.
- Surplus: up to 50% to the operator (Ju'alah). The rest goes to participants who made no claim, and can be donated to charity.
- 24-hour assistance hotline 03-2166 5421: international medical assistance, plus an admission guarantee arranged via the service provider.
